SQL Practice Quiz

10 questions on the Proprint Order System database — from a basic SELECT to a join with GROUP BY. Everyone gets the same kinds of questions, but the details are generated from your name, so your quiz is slightly different from your neighbor's.

Your name seeds the quiz: the same name always produces the same questions, so type it carefully — your instructor sees your name with your answers. If two classmates share a name, add your middle initial to make yours different.

How it works

Each question is a business question you answer with SQL. Download proprint.sql, load it into your SQL client (e.g., DB Browser for SQLite), write a query, and type the answer your query returns into the answer box. You can also paste the SQL you used — the instructor likes to see both. Answers are checked automatically when you submit, but your best move is to make sure your query really runs before you commit to an answer. Use the schema reference at the bottom of the quiz screen, and take a screenshot of your receipt when you're done.
